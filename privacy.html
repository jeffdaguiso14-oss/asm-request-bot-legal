Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Administrative Services Ministry (ASM) may process information through ASM Request Bot on the official ASM Discord server.

1. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on how you use the bot, the system may process:

  • Discord account information: your Discord user ID and username.
  • Request details: the selected ministry, representative, request type, description, and purpose.
  • Submitted files: files attached to requests and files provided by authorized reviewers.
  • Review information: request status, reviewer identity, and reasons for rejection or revision.
  • Ticket and transcript information: messages, timestamps, attachment names, and relevant embedded message content included in transcripts.

Please do not submit passwords, authentication codes, or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage private request-ticket channels.
  • Identify and contact requesters about their requests.
  • Review, approve, reject, or return requests for revision.
  • Send status notifications and deliver approved files through Discord direct messages.
  • Create and maintain administrative transcripts.
  • Troubleshoot technical issues and protect the integrity of the request system.

3. Where Information Is Processed

The bot operates through Discord and is hosted by a third-party hosting provider. Discord and the hosting provider may process information as necessary to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies.

Request transcripts are sent to the restricted 📄・request-transcripts channel on the ASM Discord server.

The bot does not automatically upload request data to Google Drive. Any separate transfer to Google Drive by an authorized person is subject to the access controls and policies applicable to that destination.

4. Access and Disclosure

Request information should be accessible only to people authorized to manage or review the request system, subject to Discord permissions, bot configuration, and the permissions of service providers.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to process a request, protect the system, address a legitimate administrative concern, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Information processed by Discord is also subject to Discord's own systems and policies.

5. Retention and Deletion

Request transcripts may be retained in the restricted transcript channel for administrative recordkeeping. This policy does not promise automatic deletion after a specific period.

Authorized ASM administrators may manage or delete records according to applicable church recordkeeping requirements and any approved retention schedule.

For questions about access, correction, or deletion of information, contact the official ASM support channel. Requests will be reviewed in light of applicable recordkeeping obligations and law.

6. Security

ASM uses access restrictions and bot permissions intended to limit access to requests and transcripts. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information and report suspected unauthorized access through the official ASM support channel.

7. Children's Information

Anyone submitting information about a child should ensure they are authorized to do so and submit only information necessary for the administrative purpose. Appropriate consent and safeguards should be obtained where required.

8. Privacy Requests and Contact

For privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete information associated with a request, contact the official ASM support channel on the ASM Discord server. Avoid posting sensitive personal information in a public channel.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the bot, its hosting arrangements, its information practices, or applicable requirements change. The current version will be published at the official Privacy Policy URL.

10. Contact

Privacy contact: Official ASM support channel on the ASM Discord server.

Operator: Administrative Services Ministry (ASM).
