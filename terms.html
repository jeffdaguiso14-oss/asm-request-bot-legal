Terms of Service

Effective date: October 9, 2026

These Terms govern your use of ASM Request Bot, an administrative tool operated by the Administrative Services Ministry (ASM) through the official ASM Discord server.

1. Purpose

ASM Request Bot receives, organizes, reviews, and processes administrative requests submitted through the official ASM Discord server. It is intended for authorized church and ministry-related administrative use.

2. Acceptance of These Terms

By using ASM Request Bot or submitting a request through it, you agree to these Terms of Service and the associated Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the bot to submit a request.

3. Acceptable Use

You agree to:

  • Submit accurate information relevant to your request.
  • Use the bot for legitimate church or ministry-related administrative purposes.
  • Upload only files you are authorized to share.
  • Communicate respectfully with authorized reviewers.
  • Avoid submitting passwords, authentication codes, unnecessary sensitive personal information, or unrelated confidential material.

You must not use the bot to submit unlawful, abusive, fraudulent, malicious, or deliberately misleading content.

4. Request Review and Decisions

Submitting a request does not guarantee approval. Authorized ASM personnel may approve a request, reject it, or ask you to provide corrections or additional information. Requests may be delayed if information is incomplete, supporting files are missing, or additional review is required.

5. Files and Administrative Records

The bot may deliver approved files through Discord direct messages and may create text transcripts of request tickets for administrative recordkeeping. Users are responsible for ensuring they have permission to submit files. Access may be available to authorized personnel who review or administer the request system.

6. Availability and Changes

The bot is provided as an administrative service. Availability may be interrupted by maintenance, hosting issues, Discord outages, technical failures, or changes to the service. ASM may modify, suspend, or discontinue the bot or any of its features when necessary.

7. Misuse and Access Restrictions

ASM may restrict access to the bot or its request system when necessary to protect the server, administrative records, users, or the integrity of the review process.

8. Privacy

Use of the bot is also governed by the ASM Request Bot Privacy Policy, which explains what information may be processed, why it is processed, and how users can raise privacy concerns.

9. Contact and Support

For questions, concerns, or requests concerning the bot, contact the official ASM support channel on the ASM Discord server. Do not post private information in public channels.

10. Changes to These Terms

ASM may update these Terms of Service when its procedures or the bot's functionality changes. The current version will be published at the official Terms of Service URL.

11. Scope

These Terms govern use of ASM Request Bot. They do not replace applicable law or other official church policies.
